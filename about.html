What this site is

Limitations

This is a case study, not a benchmark. Each point names a limit and what would lift it.

Scope

Two related planar hydrocarbons of 16 and 19 atoms were studied, with one structure each that reached the force target; larger, flexible and heteroatom-containing molecules would test how far the results transfer.

Agentic optimizer module

The pilot used one basis set, two starts per molecule and single runs per arm. The step-cap sweep is the only test that isolates the module; its 0.005 Å cap was mis-set on purpose, the arms tie at a sensible cap, and a 0.3 Å cap destroyed C11H8 in every arm. The agent largely followed a deterministic advisor, and the trap-escape actions and the basin choice were not exercised on the annulenes. A plain arm at the ASE defaults (maxstep 0.2 Å, memory 100) was run for C10H6 only, where it needed 48 steps. The rule-based controller was run on C10H6 only and, in a compact form, on ammonia and hydrogen peroxide. It did as well as the agent, so the language model is not shown to be required. A rules run on C11H8, cases outside the range of the rules, and the combined mode of rules and agent would test what the model adds. Repeated runs would measure how reproducible the agent's decisions are. Decisions depend on the model version and are not bit-reproducible, so every prompt, call and decision is logged and released.

Harmonic analysis

Frequencies are harmonic and unscaled and precise to about ±20 cm−1, and the displacement step and integration grid were not varied. Agreement with B3LYP-D3(BJ) shows consistency between two functionals, not correctness, and the spectra are not compared quantitatively with experiment. A varied step and a band-by-band comparison with the measured IR spectrum would lift these limits.

NMR

Shieldings are gas phase, whereas both constant sets were fitted with a chloroform continuum model on M06-2X (P1) and B3LYP (P2) geometries. We did not obtain usable shieldings with a continuum solvent model (section 2.4), so the effect of the solvent on these shifts is not measured here. Shieldings computed in a chloroform continuum model, with another program or a later version, would lift this limit. All four 1H MAEs are below the 0.2 ppm recommended by Parmar et al. in Nature Synthesis, but three exceed the 0.10 ppm that the Nature Protocols paper gives as typical of “correct” fits (at least 0.20 ppm being typical of “incorrect” matches), and the C10H6 13C MAE sits at the 2.0 ppm recommended by Parmar et al. in Nature Synthesis. The test shows consistency with experiment and does not rank Skala-1.1 against other functionals; constants fitted on Skala-1.1 geometries would sharpen it.

Hand-set strand

Strain was tested only from 1.0 to 4.1 eV above the minimum, one input of each kind was run per molecule and no alternative minima were sought. The CCSD(T) check covers two geometry pairs without basis-set extrapolation. More inputs and extrapolated reference energies would extend it.

Energies

All energies are electronic energies at fixed nuclei, without zero-point, thermal or entropy terms. No free energy is reported, and no statement here is about populations or reaction thermochemistry; a strain energy on the potential energy surface can differ from the corresponding difference at room temperature. Total energies of Skala-1.1 and ωB97M-V are listed in table A3 but not compared; only energy differences are, and for two geometry pairs (section 3.8). Zero-point and thermal corrections from the harmonic frequencies of module 2 would lift this limit.

What the labels mean

Every chart, animation and picture on the site carries one of four labels, so that you can tell a log from an explanation.

Every frame and value comes from a run log. Nothing is interpolated or invented.

An animation whose motion or values are not logged: a mode motion, an interpolated geometry, a state diagram or a toy curve.

A static chart rebuilt from tables of the manuscript or of the data files; not a replay of a log.

A drawing of the workflow. It is not computed data.

Use of AI

Use of AI. A language model is a run-time component of the agentic optimizer module; its role, prompts and logs are described on the Design and Code pages. The manuscript carries the authors’ full AI disclosure. This site was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed by the authors.
