Data

The data behind every replay and chart

Each file below is a run log or a table of the Note, with its level of theory and its units. The interactive pages read rounded copies of the same numbers.

Units

Forces are shown in eV Å−1 as in the manuscript (section 2.2): the logged eV bohr−1 values multiplied by 1.8897. The force target of 0.038 eV Å−1 is 0.02 in the logged unit. The data files keep the logged values.

Limit

Energies are electronic energies at fixed nuclei, on the Born–Oppenheimer surface. All runs are single runs of a small sample. The grid runs differ in more than the agent; see the caveat in the main grid.

The complete prompt, call and response logs are part of the repository release.

All files together: MANIFEST.csv lists path, size, SHA-256, description, molecule, level of theory, run and kind (logged or tabulated) for each file; README.md explains the folders.

Structures

Starting geometries R1 and R3 of each molecule, the end point of the agentic optimizer module that was handed to the Hessian and NMR workflow modules, and the geometry re-optimized for the NMR shieldings. Positions only; the comment line names the molecule and the level of theory.

Level of theory
Skala-1.1/def2-SVP (end point), Skala-1.1/6-31+G(d,p) (NMR geometry); the starts carry no level.

Trajectories of the step-cap sweep

    
For each of the 16 sweep runs (eight arms, two molecules) a multi-frame xyz with the geometry after every step (frame 0 is the start), a per-step table, and the events of the run: triggers, the decision with its written reason, the validator verdict, the five-step probation. The 0.3 Å runs of C11H8 are complete and carry structure_lost: true in the header of the events file.

Vibrational modes

    
Every vibrational mode of both molecules with Skala-1.1 at def2-TZVP against B3LYP and against Skala-1.1 at 6-31+G(d,p). In the second comparison the columns that the source labelled B3LYP hold the Skala-1.1/6-31+G(d,p) values and are renamed here. Also the labelled key modes and the energy scans along soft and control modes. No frequency is imaginary in any column.

Molecular properties

    
Relaxation energy differences of two geometry pairs against CCSD(T), CCSD, HF, ωB97M-V and Skala-1.1, and the alignment of meta-Löwdin charges. Computed on the def2-TZVP minima of the hand-set route. What a log holds

    
Two real records from the supervised run of C10H6 from the 0.005 Å cap (run C10H6_GS): one optimizer step and the decision taken at that step. 

    

      

        
One step (a row of C10H6_GS_steps.csv)

        

        

          
step
Optimizer step, counted from 1.

          
pass_id
The run is split into passes; here one pass.

          
energy_eV
Born–Oppenheimer total energy after the step.

          
fmax_logged
Largest force component in the logged unit; the target is 0.02.

          
fmax_eV_per_A
The same force times 1.8897, as in the manuscript.

          
max_move_A
Largest atom displacement in this step.

          
step_cap_A
The step cap in force during this step.

          
scf_cycles, gradients
Electronic-structure iterations of the step and the running count of gradient evaluations.

          
memory
Number of past steps that L-BFGS remembers.

        

      

      

        
One decision (an event of C10H6_GS_events.json)

        

        

          
trigger
Why the controller was asked; at an ordinary trigger it is the word “trigger”, and the monitor’s own reason sits in the preceding trigger event.

          
decision.action
One of the eight typed actions; here a setting change.

          
decision.params
The proposed values; the validator may clip them.

          
decision.rationale, evidence
The written reason and the numbers it rests on.

          
decision.expected
What the controller predicts, so that the probation can be judged.

          
tools_called
The functions the model called to reach the decision.

          
role
Which model tier answered (routine or check); model names are not kept.

          
seconds, t_s
Duration of the call and seconds since the start of the run.
