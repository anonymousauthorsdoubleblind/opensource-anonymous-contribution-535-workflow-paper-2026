Results

Nine findings, each with its limit

The findings follow the order of the manuscript, by the strength of the check applied; the comparison with experiment comes first. Each states its test, its main number, a benchmark and its limit.

Section 3.1 Module 3 · NMR workflow

Computed NMR shifts fall within published error limits

Test. ¹H and ¹³C shifts computed on the Skala-1.1 geometry (B3LYP and PBE0 shieldings), against the measured spectra.

Result. ¹³C MAE 1.30 to 1.95 ppm (limit 2.0); ¹H MAE 0.08 to 0.17 ppm (limit 0.2); all 24 resolvable signal pairs in the measured order.

1.30 to 1.95 ppm¹³C MAE (limit 2.0 ppm)

In plain terms. The computed shifts were within the error that the published limits allow.

Limit

This tests the geometry, not NMR with Skala-1.1. It does not rank Skala-1.1 against other functionals. Gas-phase shieldings; the alkyne carbons of C10H6 are 3.3 to 4.9 ppm high.

Section 3.2 Module 2 · Hessian

Both end points are true minima

Test. Number of imaginary modes in three setups; energy scans along eight modes.

Result. 0 of 42 (C10H6) and 0 of 51 (C11H8) modes imaginary with Skala-1.1/def2-TZVP, B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVP and Skala-1.1/6-31+G(d,p); 64 of 64 displaced structures above the minimum.

0 of 42 and 0 of 51modes imaginary, in three setups

In plain terms. Both structures sit on a valley floor, not on a mountain pass.

Limit

The analysis is harmonic and unscaled. Only the sign of each curvature is claimed; no comparison with a measured spectrum.

See the data

Section 3.3 Module 2 · Hessian

The finite-difference Hessian is numerically consistent

Test. Analytic force against the finite-difference slope of the energy; symmetry of the Hessian; scans against the Hessian.

Result. Ratios between 0.9991 and 1.0002; relative asymmetry 0.0011 and 0.0014; control-mode scans within 2 and 19 cm−1.

0.9991 to 1.0002analytic force over finite-difference slope

In plain terms. The energy surface was smooth enough for a finite-difference Hessian with a step of 0.01 Å, for the two molecules studied.

Limit

One step size (0.01 Å), one grid, and two rigid, planar molecules; precision about ±20 cm−1.

See the data

Section 3.4 Module 1 · agentic optimizer

Different starts and settings end at one structure

Test. 18 supervised runs with varied starts and settings, and a separately run hand-set schedule.

Result. 16 of 18 runs reached the force target, all at one structure: RMSD at most 0.016 Å (C10H6) and 0.028 Å (C11H8), energies within 1.7 meV; hand-set route within 0.004 and 0.007 Å.

16 of 18supervised runs reached the force target, at one structure

In plain terms. Every run that finished ended at the same structure.

Limit

One planar basin. Both routes share the functional and the optimizer family. The other two runs lost the C11H8 structure at a 0.3 Å cap.

Section 3.5 Module 1 · agentic optimizer

A curvature probe tells a minimum from a saddle point

Test. Ammonia inversion saddle, repeated on hydrogen peroxide; verdict checked by an independent finite-difference Hessian.

Result. 8 of 10 plain runs met the force test on the ammonia saddle; the probe reported all 7 imaginary modes it met; 6 of 6 supervised runs ended in a minimum; hydrogen peroxide: 0 of 3 plain, 3 of 3 supervised.

6 of 6supervised ammonia runs ended in a minimum (plain: 8 of 10 stopped on the saddle)

In plain terms. The force test alone accepted a mountain pass as a valley floor. The curvature probe did not.

Limit

Two small test molecules, not annulenes; a compact version of the module. A rule-based supervisor without a language model did as well. No imaginary mode appeared on the annulenes.

Section 3.6 Module 1 · agentic optimizer

Supervised runs recover from a step cap set too small

Test. Runs started on purpose with a step cap of 0.005 Å; first step below the force target.

Result. Supervised runs: steps 58 to 70. Plain runs: step 129, or not within 149 steps. A rules run without a language model: step 50 (C10H6).

58 to 70steps to the force target, supervised runs

In plain terms. The supervised runs raised a cap that was set too small and then reached the force target.

Limit

At a sensible cap the two tied, and at the ASE defaults a plain run took 48 steps. A 0.3 Å cap destroyed C11H8. Single runs; no speed-up is claimed.

Section 3.7 Module 1 · agentic optimizer

Every agent decision stays within bounds and is logged

Test. Number of proposals that reached the optimizer outside the bounds of the validator.

Result. Of 208 decisions, 192 passed and 16 were clipped to the bounds; none fell outside them. 72 of 78 trial changes kept, 6 rolled back. 341 model calls, none failed.

0 of 208proposals outside the bounds (192 passed, 16 clipped)

In plain terms. Only checked and reversible changes reached the optimizer. Every decision was recorded.

Limit

The agent agreed with the step advisor in 81 of 97 decisions, so these runs do not show what the language model adds. Runs are not bit-reproducible.

Section 3.8 Module 4 · molecular property

Charges and relaxation energies sit beside established methods

Test. Meta-Löwdin charges against ωB97M-V; relaxation energy of two geometry pairs against CCSD(T)/def2-TZVP.

Result. Charges: R² above 0.999. Relaxation energy deviations from CCSD(T): −0.40 (Skala-1.1) and +0.36 (ωB97M-V) kcal mol−1 for C11H8; +3.13 and +4.25 kcal mol−1 for C10H6.

R² above 0.999meta-Löwdin charges against ωB97M-V

In plain terms. The Skala-1.1 charges matched those of an established hybrid functional. The relaxation energies are shown side by side, without a ranking.

Limit

Two geometry pairs only. Not a statement of chemical accuracy and not a ranking. Computed on the hand-set minima.

See the data

Section 3.9 Run record

The workflow runs end to end on single cloud GPUs

Test. The four modules run in sequence for both annulenes; run time and peak GPU memory.

Result. def2-TZVP gradient 9.9 s and 11.4 s; Hessian with IR intensities 16.1 and 22.0 min (97 and 115 gradients) on one A100; peak memory 7.1 and 8.4 GB; property single point 149 s on a T4.

16.1 and 22.0 minHessian with IR intensities on one A100

In plain terms. Every GPU stage ran on a single rented GPU.

Limit

Single runs on shared cloud GPUs, for molecules of 16 and 19 atoms. Not a comparison of cost with other functionals. The median model call took 9.3 s, comparable to one def2-SVP gradient, so step counts should not be read as savings of wall time.

What the results do not establish

The results do not establish the accuracy of Skala-1.1 in general, its cost relative to conventional functionals, the benefit of supervision beyond this pilot, or that a language model is needed for it. Each result holds under the conditions stated with it: two related hydrocarbons, single runs, harmonic frequencies, gas-phase shieldings and two coupled-cluster geometry pairs (section 6).
