The hand-set route

The hand-set schedule is the separately run reference for the agent-supervised route. It has four tiers: (1) a coarse relaxation with GFN2-xTB; (2) relaxation with the MACE machine-learned interatomic potential; (3) a Skala-1.1/def2-SVP optimization with L-BFGS in three passes; (4) a Skala-1.1/def2-TZVP optimization in three further passes. Randomly deformed inputs pass through all four tiers, and strained inputs enter at tier 3 (section 2.5).

Relaxation energy: signed deviation from CCSD(T)

Bars in a fixed order (not sorted by size); the two functionals share one neutral fill with different patterns. Data: assets/data/props.js

ΔE = E(strained) − E(relaxed). One geometry pair per molecule (Kabsch RMSD 0.151 Å for C₁₁H₈, 0.235 Å for C₁₀H₆). Frozen-core CCSD(T)/def2-TZVP, no basis-set extrapolation.

Energies are electronic energies at fixed nuclei, on the Born–Oppenheimer surface. No zero-point, thermal or entropy term is added, and no free energy is reported.

Limit

A case study of two geometry pairs: not a statement of chemical accuracy and not a ranking of the two functionals. The triples correction of the reference is 2.05 and 4.38 kcal mol−1, so the reference itself is sensitive to high-order correlation. The relaxed geometry of each pair came from a Skala-1.1 optimization. The integration grids and dispersion treatments of the two functionals were not matched. The two pairs differ in molecule and in the size of the distortion (section 3.8).
