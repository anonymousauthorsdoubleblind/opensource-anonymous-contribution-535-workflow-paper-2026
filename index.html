What is new, and how far that statement goes

To our knowledge, four things had not been reported (searches of Google Scholar, arXiv, ChemRxiv, the Skala repository and the web, run on 10 to 13 November 2026):

  1. No harmonic frequencies, infrared intensities or vibrational densities of states of an annulene computed with Skala-1.1 have been published.
  2. No Skala-optimized annulene geometry has been tested against the experimental 1H and 13C NMR spectrum of the synthesized compound.
  3. To our knowledge, no earlier work has a large language model agent, through function calling with typed actions, supervise the step and convergence settings of a running Skala-1.1/L-BFGS geometry optimization behind a validator, a probation window and rollback of the running optimization.
  4. No publication independently evaluates Skala's release of high steric strain (more than 1 eV) in an annulene geometry optimization.
Limits on these statements

Adapting the step size during a run is standard practice in optimizers, and rule-based supervision of a run is not claimed as new. Most elements of the third statement have precedent on their own, so it rests on their combination. It does not say that a language model is needed for the supervision, which the Note does not show. The searches were bounded, so the absence of earlier work in them is not proof that none exists.

For the optimizer, the contribution is an interface in which every change is a typed, bounded, reversible and logged action, which any controller, rule-based or model-based, can use. The benefit is attributed to the supervision layer, that is, the monitor, the step advisor and the guard, and not to the language model. A rule-based controller did as well where we tested it, so a language model is not shown to be required.

Five numbers, each with its limit

The values are those of the manuscript, as on the Results page. Each tile links to the replay or table behind it.

1.30 to 1.95 ppm 13C mean absolute error against a published limit of 2.0 ppm; 1H 0.08 to 0.17 ppm against 0.2 ppm

Limit: This tests the geometry, not NMR with Skala-1.1. Gas-phase shieldings.

NMR errors →
0 of 42 and 0 of 51 modes imaginary for C10H6 and C11H8: both end points are minima

Limit: Harmonic and unscaled; only the sign of each curvature is claimed.

Frequencies and spectrum →
16 of 18 supervised runs reached the force target, all at one structure (RMSD at most 0.016 Å and 0.028 Å)

Limit: One planar basin. The other two runs lost the C11H8 structure at a 0.3 Å cap.

One structure →
Steps 58 to 70 to the force target for supervised runs started on purpose at a 0.005 Å step cap; plain runs: step 129, or not within 149 steps; a rules run without a language model: step 50

Limit: At a sensible cap the two tied. Single runs, given as step counts. No gain in speed is claimed.

Step-cap sweep →
208 decisions 192 passed and 16 were clipped to the bounds; none fell outside them. 72 of 78 trial changes kept, 6 rolled back

Limit: The agent agreed with the step advisor in 81 of 97 decisions, so these runs do not show what the language model adds.
